### Enter Your Second Life
You once belonged to another world. Now, a mysterious ritual originating from beyond the boundaries of worlds has brought you into the Cardinal World, granting you a second life in an unfamiliar reality. Your existence is an anomaly within the World System: a life that has emerged through a process capable of altering the very conditions by which the Cardinal World defines what may exist.

The aformentioned ritual was no conventional summoning or reincarnation—after all, at its heart lies an echo of Star-King Dragon Veldanava's 『**Paradise Genesis: Beginning Eden**』. As a result, your existence is not entirely bound by the rules that govern the Cardinal World; you possess an unusual connection to the World System, one that opens possibilities unavailable to ordinary beings...

Through this connection, your second life need not be confined to the boundaries of a single world. You may be reborn as the race you belonged to in your previous life, choose a race native to the Cardinal World, or even take the form of a species originating from another world connected through the World System. Whatever form you choose, your journey will be one of discovering your potential, uncovering the depths of your abilities, and learning what it means to exist.

### Choose Your Origin
Before deciding what you will become, consider where your previous life began. The world you called home need not have been Earth. Perhaps you were an ordinary human whose life unfolded beneath its familiar sky, with no knowledge of the extraordinary realities beyond it. Perhaps you belonged to another world entirely, one with its own civilizations, histories, and fundamental laws, such as Disboard or Kasanagara. Or perhaps your first life was already rooted in the Cardinal World, and your rebirth has brought you back to a reality you once knew, though not necessarily to a life or place you recognize.

Your origin is yours to determine. It establishes the world from which you came, the life you left behind, and the perspective you carry into this new existence. It need not dictate the race you become, nor must it confine you to the customs or expectations of your former home. An Earthling may be reborn as a monster of the Cardinal World; a being from another reality may find themselves inhabiting a body shaped by entirely different laws; and someone who once lived in the Cardinal World may discover that returning to a familiar world is no guarantee of returning to a familiar life.

The possibilities extend beyond simply choosing where your story began. Consider what you remember; and what you have lost.

### Claim Your Destiny
This is your second life, and with it comes a beginning unconstrained by any predetermined destiny. The World System has opened a path that should never have existed, but it has made no promise concerning where that path will lead. What you build, what you uncover, and what you become will depend on the choices you make.

At the crossroads of the Cardinal World and beyond, the races, powers, and laws of different realities will all converge.

——This is 【**Tensura Cross Ultima**】.
